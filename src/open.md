We reviewed the privacy services advertised by New Zealand consultancies, accounting and advisory firms, cyber security companies and law firms. This table maps the common services to free alternatives. Good advisers add real value. This page is here so you can decide what you need to buy.

<div class="table-scroll">

| Service commonly sold | Free alternative | Pay a professional when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy policy or statement drafting | [Privacy statement template](/templates/privacy-statement/), [collection notices](/templates/collection-notices/), the Commissioner's [Privacy Statement Generator](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/privacy-statement-generator/) | You operate in several countries or under several regimes at once |
| Privacy gap analysis or audit | [Privacy self-check](/tools/privacy-self-check/), [90-day programme](/guides/organisations/first-90-days/) | A board, funder or client needs independent assurance |
| Privacy maturity assessment | [Privacy self-check](/tools/privacy-self-check/); for government, the [PMAF](https://www.digital.govt.nz/standards-and-guidance/privacy-security-and-risk/privacy/privacy-maturity-assessment-framework-pmaf-and-self-assessments/learn-about-pmaf-2/what-pmaf-is) | You need benchmarking against other organisations |
| Privacy impact assessment | [PIA template](/templates/privacy-impact-assessment/), [biometric worksheet](/templates/biometric-assessment/) | The project is high-risk: biometrics, AI decisions about people, large-scale data matching, children, health |
| Data mapping and inventory | [Inventory and interview guide](/templates/data-inventory/) | You have hundreds of systems and need tooling |
| Retention schedules | [Retention schedule](/templates/retention-schedule/) | You're subject to the Public Records Act or complex sector rules |
| Breach response planning | [Breach response plan and register](/templates/breach-response-plan/), [breach assessment record](/tools/breach/) | You want a facilitated exercise with experienced incident responders |

| Supplier or third-party risk review | [Supplier questionnaire](/templates/vendor-questionnaire/) | The supplier is critical and you need technical security testing |
| Staff training | [30-minute induction](/templates/staff-induction/), the Commissioner's free [e-learning](https://www.privacy.org.nz/resources-and-learning/online-privacy-training-free/) | You need role-specific training for high-risk teams |

## Free help from public bodies

- **Office of the Privacy Commissioner**: guidance including Poupou Matatapu, NotifyUs for breaches, e-learning, and an enquiries line.
- **National Cyber Security Centre**: incident reporting and help on 0800 114 115, and Own Your Online for individuals and small businesses.
- **Netsafe**: online harm, scams and harmful digital communications, on 0508 638 723.
- **Community Law centres**: free legal help, including on privacy and harassment.
- **Citizens Advice Bureau**: free advice and referrals.

## Before you hire anyone

Do the [self-check](/tools/privacy-self-check/) and the [inventory](/templates/data-inventory/) first. You'll know what you need, you'll spend less, and you'll be able to tell a good adviser from a template seller.
