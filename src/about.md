Faraday's work was with high-risk people and organisations, protecting their online identity and digital privacy. That included people at risk because of who they are, what they do for a living, or the information they handle. The work covered digital privacy, online safety, information security, data governance and reputation, sometimes planned well in advance and often in a crisis. This site is what that work taught us, written down for anyone to use.

Faraday was founded in Auckland by {{ site.founder }}, whose work brings together law and policy, information security, digital privacy and Māori data sovereignty.

## Why the name

A Faraday cage is a metal mesh that controls which electromagnetic signals get in and out. Good privacy does the same for information about you.

## Why it's free

We've published what we know as an open commons for Aotearoa, with guides, tools and templates anyone can use and adapt. See [why we're giving it away](/blog/giving-it-away/).

## How the site is made

- **Grounded in primary sources.** Every legal claim links to the legislation or the regulator's guidance, with the date we last checked it. See [sources and checks](/sources/).
- **Written for New Zealand.** New Zealand law, New Zealand agencies, New Zealand phone numbers, New Zealand English.
- **Honest about limits.** We tell you when you need a lawyer, an incident responder or an advocate. See [free versus paid](/open/).
- **Practising what we preach.** This site has no analytics, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts, and a strict content security policy. See [our privacy statement](/privacy/).
- **Careful with dual-use knowledge.** Advice about what's public about a person can help someone protect themselves, or help someone find them. We write from the defender's side, and our [editorial standards]({{ site.repo }}/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) set out how we decide.

## Using this information

This site is general information, not advice. Each page shows the date its sources were last checked, so check anything important against the source before you rely on it. To report an error, use the link at the bottom of any page.

For help with your own situation, contact the [Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/), [Netsafe](https://netsafe.org.nz/) or your local [Community Law centre](https://communitylaw.org.nz/). If something is happening now, go to [Get help now](/help/).
