## How to use this

The Privacy Commissioner's first pou is governance, meaning leaders who understand and oversee privacy risk. A short report twice a year is the simplest way to show that. Keep it to two pages. Lead with the decisions you need from the board.

<!-- template -->

# Privacy report to [the board / leadership team]

**Period:** [1 January to 30 June 2027]
**Prepared by:** [privacy officer]
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Decisions needed

[List anything the board needs to decide or approve: budget, accepting a risk, approving the privacy plan. If none, say so.]
