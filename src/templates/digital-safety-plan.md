## How to use this

This worksheet is for you, and ideally for working through with a family violence advocate. Women's Refuge (**0800 733 843**, 24 hours) and Shine (**0508 744 633**) help with technology safety as part of safety planning.

**Keep it somewhere the other person can't find it.** A paper copy in a safe place, or a file on a device or account they've never had access to. If you download it, think about where the file will be saved and synced.

Read the [technology-facilitated abuse guide](/guides/people/tech-abuse/) first. It explains why the order of changes matters.

## 5. Changes, in order

Decide with your advocate what to change, when, and what to do if they react.

| Change | When | Could they notice? | What I'll do if they do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | |

Things people often do first, quietly: set up a safe email; move important documents to it; save evidence; check bank accounts. Things people often do later, at a planned time: remove location sharing; change passwords and recovery details; get a new phone.

## 6. Reducing what's public

- [ ] Ask my council to withhold my name from the rates database

## 7. If I'm in danger

Call **111**. If I can't speak, call 111 and press **55** when asked.
