# Code of conduct

This project exists to protect people. Treat everyone involved in it the way the project asks the world to treat them.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially with people new to privacy or to open source.
- Critique content and sources, not people.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post anyone's personal information in issues, pull requests or discussions, including your own if it could put you at risk.
- Remember that some contributors and readers have lived experience of abuse, stalking or harassment.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, slurs or personal attacks.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information.
- Sexualised content or imagery.
- Using this project to plan, request or share techniques for locating, tracking or monitoring a specific person.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu on the comment, issue or pull request, and choose to report it to the repository's maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially. We may remove content, block accounts or take other action we judge appropriate.
