# Asking an expert to review

Copy and adapt. Expert review is one of the ways we know the content is right, and most practitioners will help a free public resource if asked specifically.

---

**Subject:** Would you review one page of a free privacy resource for New Zealanders?

Kia ora [name],

I'm [your name] from Faraday. We've published a free, open set of privacy and online safety guides for New Zealand at faraday.co.nz. Everything is cited to primary sources, but we want practitioners to check it.

Would you be willing to review [one page: link] against your experience? It takes about 20 minutes. We're looking for:

- anything wrong or out of date
- anything misleading by omission
- anything that could put a reader at risk

You can reply by email, or comment on GitHub if you prefer: [link to issue]. With your permission we'll acknowledge your review on the page; or we can keep it anonymous.

Ngā mihi,
[name]
